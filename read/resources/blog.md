# Blog

Posts from the ReliefWeb blog ([reliefweb.int/blog](https://reliefweb.int/blog)) about ReliefWeb features, updates, and projects (`title`, `author`, `body`, `tags`, `date`).

- **Endpoint:** `POST` or `GET https://api.reliefweb.int/v2/blog`
- **Single post:** `GET https://api.reliefweb.int/v2/blog/{id|uuid}`
